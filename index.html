HIGH-PRECISION DIAGNOSTIC ULTRASOUND

Advanced Imaging.
Clearer Answers.

Equipped with high-resolution diagnostic ultrasound technology, Ibn Sina Hospital provides precise 2D, 3D, and 4D imaging alongside advanced Color Doppler scans for fetal assessment, gynecology, abdominal, and renal evaluations.

2D Imaging 3D Fetal Visualization 4D Real-time Motion Color Doppler Studies

Obstetric USG (2D / 3D / 4D)

Detailed fetal growth monitoring, anatomical anomaly screening, and 3D/4D visualization of your baby in the womb.

Color Doppler Imaging

Advanced vascular flow assessments for umbilical arteries, fetal circulation, and peripheral venous or arterial evaluations.

Abdomen & Pelvic USG

High-clarity evaluation of liver, gallbladder, kidney, pancreas, spleen, uterus, and ovaries.

Renal & KUB USG

Targeted ultrasound imaging for kidneys, ureters, and bladder to identify kidney stones, blockages, or infections.

Thyroid & Neck Scan

Precision soft tissue imaging for thyroid nodules, swelling, and cervical lymph nodes.
